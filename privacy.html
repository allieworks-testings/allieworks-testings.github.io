Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account, broadcast a stream, upload videos, or participate in live chat on YouFry, we collect certain information necessary for platform functionality. This includes your username, account credentials, unique stream keys, chat room messages, and connection telemetry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to operate, maintain, and secure the YouFry streaming backend. Specifically, your data is used to authenticate your login sessions, manage real-time active viewer counts, track followed channels, and synchronize chat logs across users.

3. AI Moderation & Automated Processing

To ensure platform safety, uploaded media and broadcasts may be processed through automated AI moderation pipelines to check for prohibited or NSFW content. Telemetry and moderation results are handled dynamically to enforce site rules.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account credentials and application data are securely stored in our backend database. While we take standard technical measures to safeguard your information, no transmission over the internet or storage method is completely secure.

5. Local Storage & Session State

YouFry uses browser local storage to maintain your active login session and user identity across pages. We do not use intrusive third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may modify this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of YouFry after any updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
